About Us

Parking Made Easy, Travel
Made Better

Travel is stressful enough without worrying about where to leave your car. That's why Joy9 Park exists — to give travellers a safe, affordable place to park just minutes from JFK.

Our secure parking lot in Jamaica, NY has been serving airport travellers for years with round-the-clock access, barrier-controlled entry and a free shuttle that runs to every terminal. Whether you're away for a weekend or a month, your vehicle stays protected until the moment you return.

How It Works

From the moment you book to the moment you drive away, the whole process takes just four simple steps. No paperwork queues, no shuttle waiting around and no surprises when you come back to collect your car.

  • Step 01. Reserve

    Book online or call ahead to secure your parking spot for the dates you need.

  • Step 02. Arrive

    Drive straight to our lot, check in at the gate and hand over your keys.

  • Step 03. Shuttle

    Hop on our free shuttle and we'll have you at your terminal in 5 to 7 minutes.

  • Step 04. Return

    Call us once you land and the shuttle will collect you and bring you back to your car.
